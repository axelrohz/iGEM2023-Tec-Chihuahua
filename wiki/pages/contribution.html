We share our contributions to Igem community

Contribution

We know that iGEM has great value as a source of knowledge and development of synthetic biology, and it is thanks to the huge community that forms it. To be completely sure that we are part of this great family, we have left an important part of our experience throughout the competition.

Through this page, we share our contributions to the iGEM community, hoping that they can be useful for future generations. Our contribution is made up of three main documents: Taking into account the experience of past generations of our group, we decided to develop a business plan guide to share with the community all the knowledge, recommendations and steps to successfully develop a business plan and transform projects into startups.

In the same way, we decided to take as a guide one of the most important areas of the competition, the laboratory. In our manual, "What to do when you really don't know what to do?" we have captured all of our learning in a problem-solving form. Finally, hoping that our documentation may be of use to future teams interested in the use of endolysins, or bovine mastitis, we have registered new basic and compound parts.


Business Plan Guide

Developing a business plan can be tricky and at times confusing. This is why we are delighted to offer this handbook for upcoming iGEM teams, thus facilitating the process of becoming entrepreneurs!
