Introduction

In the biggest state of Mexico, Chihuahua, lies our tight-knit team, bound not only by our passion for science but also by our deep love for our community. Each one of us has a unique connection to this land, whether we were born here or have spent most of our lives embracing its breathtaking landscapes and basking in the warmth of its people. This beautiful state thrives on its economy driven by mining, agriculture, and livestock sectors. The determination and hard work of its people has been the lifeblood of our local economy, shaping the fundamental structure of our society.


In the early stages of our project, we pondered the possible challenges to tackle, and one of our teammates, Alexa, shared a story about the issues her godmother, Marcela Hernández, a woman who owns a dairy farm, faced. This sparked our curiosity, and we embarked on an online investigation into the world of dairy farming. During our research, a pressing issue captured our attention – mastitis, a prevalent problem in the dairy industry. We became aware of the significant financial losses that this disease caused for dairy cattle owners, a revelation that awakened us to the gravity of the issue, and we felt compelled to explore this issue further.
