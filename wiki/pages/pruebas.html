Proyect Description


FOOD SAFETY STARTS ON THE FARM


AureoBos’ Origin


In the biggest state of Mexico, Chihuahua, lies our tight-knit team, bound not only by our passion for science but also by our love for our community. Our connection to the region, rich in stunning landscapes and warm people, drives our work. The state's economy thrives on mining, agriculture, and livestock activities, with the determination of its residents shaping society's core. During the project's early stages, inspired by a story from our teammate Alexa about her godmother's dairy farm challenges, we delved into dairy farming issues. Mastitis, a prevalent disease affecting dairy cattle, caught our attention due to its significant multifaceted impact on dairy owners. Motivated by Chihuahua's prominence in the Mexican dairy industry [1], we recognized the global significance of addressing this issue. Analyzing the problems that impact the world, we sought to understand how our community is bound to the international economic activities, and we found the perfect candidate: milk. Milk is a nutrient-dense food that plays an important role in the diet of over 6 billion people around the world. It provides high-quality protein and essential micronutrients in an easily absorbed form (figure 1) [2].

We aim to contribute to our feeding’s backbone by offering a safe solution to one of the biggest problems of the milk production chain. United with the same purpose, we work in an attempt to reduce food waste and improve quality as well as enhance food safety. This way, AureoBos reveals a new possibility to revolutionize the dairy industry.


Into the Udder / Impact of Bovine Mastitis


When we think about milk, we immediately associate it with cows, and that is not very far from reality. In fact, 82% of total world milk is obtained from cattle [3]. About 150 million households worldwide are engaged in dairy farming. In most developing countries, like Mexico, milk production is carried out by smallholders, exerting a significant impact on household livelihoods, food security and nutritional requirements. For these smallholders, dairy farming offers a crucial source of income [4]. Did you know… With an astonishing production of 550,498 thousand metric tons of milk as of July 2023[5], the dairy industry stands a major employer worldwide, underscoring the critical importance of udder health.
