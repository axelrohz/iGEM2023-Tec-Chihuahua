Healthy Udders, no antibiotics: The Power of endolysins

Please see the 2023 Medals Page for more information.

What is it about?

    Bacteriophage endolysins are considered a great alternative for antibiotics since they have shown specific antimicrobial activity against gram positive bacteria, even in antibiotic resistant strains.Using the power of endolysins, we approach the bovine mastitis problem caused by two gram-positive bacterias: Staphylococcus aureus and Streptococcus agalactiae. Endolysins interact with the bacterial cell membrane, causing cellular lysis. These proteins are composed of two main domains: N-terminal part, which presents the catalytic domain, and C-terminal part, which is the cell wall binding domain. This last domain determines the specificity of the endolysin against bacterias (Fernández et al., 2017). Nonetheless, the endolysins half-life approaches 20 minutes. For this reason, we look forward to increasing its half-life by binding the protein to albumin. In this project we use two endolysins: LysCSA13 and LysK.
